Our Story & Mission

Fifteen years of compassionate care, medical excellence, and unwavering dedication to every patient who opens their door to us.

Our Journey

From a Seed of Compassion to India's Premier Home Care

CareHaven was born in 2010 when Dr. Priya Nair, a senior ICU nurse, witnessed her own grandmother struggle to recover from surgery in a hospital environment when all she wanted was to be home. That personal experience ignited a mission: to bring hospital-quality care into the warmth and familiarity of home.

What started as a small team of 5 dedicated nurses in Chennai has grown into South India's most trusted home nursing agency — with 200+ certified caregivers, operations in 7 cities, and over 5,000 families served with the highest standards of medical and compassionate care.

Today, every member of the CareHaven family is united by a single belief: that compassionate, dignified care is not a luxury — it is a right that every patient and family deserves.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional, compassionate home healthcare that restores health, preserves dignity, and enriches the quality of life for every patient we serve — making professional care accessible to all.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted home nursing brand — where every family has access to hospital-quality care without leaving the comfort and security of their own home.

Our Promise

We promise to treat every patient as if they were our own family — with the same medical expertise, the same personal attention, and the same fierce dedication to their wellbeing and recovery.

We lead with empathy, always listening to the patient's voice and responding to their emotional and psychological needs alongside their clinical ones.
