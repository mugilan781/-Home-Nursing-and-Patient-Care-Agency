Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2025 • Effective: August 25, 2025

Contents

Summary: CareHaven collects and uses your personal and medical information solely to provide and improve our home nursing services. We treat your medical data with the highest level of confidentiality and never sell your information to third parties. We comply fully with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023.

1. Introduction

CareHaven Home Nursing & Patient Care ("CareHaven," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and the confidentiality of your personal and health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our website (carehaven.in), mobile application, or healthcare services.

By accessing our website or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Identification Information

  • Full name, date of birth, gender, and contact details
  • Home and mailing address
  • Phone numbers (primary, emergency contacts)
  • Email address
  • Government ID numbers (for caregiver background verification)
  • Insurance policy numbers and provider information

2.2 Medical and Health Information

  • Medical history, diagnoses, and treatment records
  • Surgical and hospitalization records
  • Current medications, allergies, and adverse reactions
  • Vital signs and health monitoring data collected during care visits
  • Treating physician information and care instructions
  • Home environment and safety assessment data

2.3 Technical Information

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent on site, and navigation patterns
  • Cookie identifiers and session data
  • Form submission records

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Care provision: Matching appropriate caregivers, developing care plans, and delivering nursing services
  • Communication: Contacting you about appointments, care updates, and service changes
  • Billing: Processing payments and managing insurance claims
  • Safety: Emergency response, caregiver coordination, and incident reporting
  • Quality improvement: Reviewing care outcomes and improving our services
  • Legal compliance: Meeting our regulatory and reporting obligations under Indian healthcare law
  • Marketing: Sending relevant health information and service updates (only with explicit consent)

4. Medical Information — Special Protections

Your medical information receives the highest level of protection under our policies and Indian law. We apply additional safeguards beyond those required for general personal data.

All medical information is:

  • Stored in encrypted, access-controlled systems separate from general data
  • Accessible only to authorized clinical staff involved in your care
  • Never shared with employers, insurers (without consent), or family members without explicit authorization
  • Protected against unauthorized access through strict role-based access controls
  • Retained only as long as medically and legally necessary

5. Information Sharing

CareHaven does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Your treating physicians: To ensure coordinated, safe medical care
  • Our caregivers: Only clinical information necessary to provide your requested services
  • Insurance providers: For claims processing, with your explicit consent
  • Legal authorities: When required by law, court order, or to prevent imminent harm
  • Service providers: Trusted technology partners bound by strict confidentiality agreements (e.g., secure cloud storage, communications)

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • AES-256 encryption for stored sensitive data
  • Multi-factor authentication for all staff system access
  • Regular penetration testing and security audits
  • ISO 27001-aligned information security practices
  • Staff training on data protection and privacy compliance

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable healthcare regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal and medical information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Request data portability in a machine-readable format
  • File a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@carehaven.in.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience. We use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics)
  • Preference cookies: Remember your theme and language settings
  • Marketing cookies: Used only with your explicit consent for relevant care information

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information according to the following schedule:

  • Active patient records: Duration of care + 7 years (Indian medical records requirement)
  • Financial records: 7 years (Indian tax law compliance)
  • Website analytics data: 26 months
  • Marketing data: Until consent is withdrawn
  • Job applicant data: 6 months if unsuccessful

10. Children's Privacy

Our services may include care for minors. Where we collect information about children (under 18), we require explicit parental or guardian consent and apply enhanced protections to all such data. Parents or guardians may request access, correction, or deletion of their child's information at any time.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make significant changes, we will notify registered users by email and display a prominent notice on our website. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
