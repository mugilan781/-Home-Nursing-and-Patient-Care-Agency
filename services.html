Post-Surgery Home Care

Returning home after surgery is a critical transition. Our specialized post-operative nursing team ensures a safe, comfortable, and complication-free recovery — bringing ICU-standard care right to your bedroom.

Wound assessment, cleaning, and professional dressing changes
IV line management and catheter care
Vital signs monitoring (BP, temperature, SpO2, pulse)
Post-surgical medication administration
Mobility assistance and fall prevention
Physician liaison and discharge planning support
Family education on home care protocols

Physiotherapy Assistance

Our certified physiotherapy assistants work under the supervision of registered physiotherapists to deliver prescribed therapeutic exercises, mobility training, and pain management directly in your home — accelerating recovery and improving functional independence.

Doctor-prescribed exercise plan implementation
Stroke rehabilitation and neurological recovery support
Post-fracture and joint replacement mobility training
Gait training and balance improvement
TENS therapy and pain management techniques
Respiratory physiotherapy for pulmonary conditions
Progress documentation and physician reporting

🩹 Service 04

Wound Dressing & Care

Proper wound management at home significantly reduces hospitalization risk and accelerates healing. Our trained nurses use sterile techniques and medical-grade materials to manage all types of wounds — from surgical incisions to diabetic foot ulcers.

Comprehensive wound assessment and documentation
Sterile wound cleaning and debridement (where indicated)
Professional dressing application and change
Infection surveillance and early warning detection
Diabetic foot ulcer specialized care
Pressure sore prevention and management
Wound photography and physician reporting

💊 Service 05

Medication Management

Medication errors are among the most common and preventable causes of patient harm. Our certified nurses implement rigorous medication safety protocols to ensure every dose is administered correctly, on time, and with full documentation.

Accurate dose preparation and administration
Prescription adherence monitoring and tracking
Multi-drug interaction surveillance
Pharmacy coordination and prescription refills
Adverse reaction identification and reporting
Insulin administration for diabetic patients
Detailed medication log maintenance

Why Home Care

Home Care vs. Hospital Stay

Studies consistently show that patients recover faster, more comfortably, and with fewer complications in a familiar home environment with professional nursing support.

Factor CareHaven Home Care Hospital Stay
Comfort Level ⭐ Home environment, familiar surroundings Clinical, institutional setting
Risk of Infection ✓ Significantly lower (no hospital pathogens) Higher (HAI risk: 5–10%)
Family Involvement ✓ Continuous, unrestricted Restricted visiting hours
Recovery Speed ✓ 30–40% faster (proven studies) Standard recovery
Cost ✓ 60–70% more cost-effective High institutional cost
