How quickly can CareHaven send a caregiver to my home?

+

For urgent requests, we can typically deploy a qualified caregiver within 4–6 hours of your inquiry. For planned care, we recommend booking 24–48 hours in advance to ensure the perfect caregiver match. Our emergency line is available 24/7.

Are your caregivers medically certified?

+

Yes. All our caregivers hold relevant medical certifications — GNM (General Nursing & Midwifery), B.Sc Nursing, or equivalent qualifications. Each has undergone our 120-hour internal training program and passed our 6-stage background verification process.

Can I meet the caregiver before starting care?

+

Absolutely. We strongly encourage a meet-and-greet session before care begins. This allows the patient and family to get comfortable with the caregiver, and allows us to fine-tune the care plan based on the caregiver's initial assessment.

What if I'm not satisfied with the assigned caregiver?

+

Your satisfaction is our priority. If at any point you feel a caregiver is not the right fit — for any reason — simply contact your care coordinator and we will arrange a replacement within 24 hours. No questions asked.

Is CareHaven covered by health insurance?

+

Many health insurance plans cover home nursing services under their domiciliary care provisions. We work with all major insurance providers and will assist you with documentation for reimbursement claims. Contact us for insurer-specific guidance.

What cities do you currently serve?

+

We currently operate in Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune, and Coimbatore. We are rapidly expanding to 20+ cities by 2026. Contact us even if your city isn't listed — we may be able to arrange care in your area.
