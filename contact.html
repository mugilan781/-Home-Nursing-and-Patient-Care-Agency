Quick Answers

Before You Contact Us

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

+

All inquiries submitted via our form or email receive a response within 2 hours during office hours (8am–8pm, Mon–Sat). For urgent care needs or emergencies, please call our 24/7 helpline directly for immediate assistance.

Do I need to pay anything for the initial consultation?

+

Absolutely not. Our initial care needs assessment and consultation is completely free of charge. A care coordinator will visit your home or conduct a thorough phone assessment at no cost or obligation.

Can I speak to a nurse directly before booking?

+

Yes. You can request a direct conversation with one of our senior clinical nurses before making any commitment. Simply call our helpline and ask to speak with a clinical advisor — they can answer specific medical questions about your loved one's care needs.

I'm not sure what service I need. Can you help?

+

Absolutely. Our care coordinators are trained to assess your situation and recommend the most appropriate level of care. Simply describe the patient's condition and what challenges you're facing — we'll guide you to the right solution.
