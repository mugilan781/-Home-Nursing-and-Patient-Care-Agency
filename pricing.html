Pricing Questions

Common Billing & Payment Questions

Questions about our pricing, payment methods, or insurance claims? Our billing team is here to help.

Contact Billing Team

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major payment methods: cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS), and all major debit/credit cards including EMI options for monthly plans.

Is there a minimum booking duration?

+

For hourly visits, the minimum booking is 3 hours. This ensures the caregiver has adequate time to complete all necessary clinical procedures and documentation without rushing.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

+

Yes. Cancellations with 24+ hours notice are fully refunded or rescheduled at no charge. For same-day cancellations, a 50% session fee applies to compensate the caregiver for their reserved time.

Are there additional charges for night shifts?

+

Night shifts (9pm–6am) carry a 25% premium on the standard hourly rate. This is disclosed upfront during booking and reflected clearly in your invoice.

How do monthly plan discounts work?

+

Monthly plans are pre-paid 30-day packages at discounted rates (15–25% off equivalent daily rates). Unused days due to patient hospitalization can be carried forward or refunded on request.
