How We Select & Train Our Caregivers

Only the top 5% of applicants make it through our rigorous selection process. Here's how we ensure every caregiver who enters your home is truly exceptional.

1

Application & Initial Screening

Candidates submit credentials, qualifications, and references. Our clinical team reviews each application against our strict qualification benchmarks.

2

Background Verification (6-Stage)

Criminal record check, identity verification, address verification, reference calls, medical fitness assessment, and educational credential verification.

3

Clinical Skills Assessment

Hands-on evaluation of clinical competencies by our senior nurses — covering vital monitoring, wound care, medication safety, and emergency response.

4

Soft Skills & Empathy Training

60-hour program covering patient communication, cultural sensitivity, dignity of care, and working with vulnerable populations including dementia patients.

5

CareHaven Protocol Training

Comprehensive 60-hour training on our proprietary care protocols, documentation systems, safety checklists, and emergency escalation procedures.

6

Continuous Education Program

Mandatory 120+ hours of annual continuing education to stay current with medical advances, new care protocols, and specialist certification renewals.
