How It Works

Simple Steps to
Expert Home Care

Getting started with CareHaven is straightforward. Our care coordinators guide you through every step, ensuring a seamless transition to professional home care — typically within 24 hours of your inquiry.

Start Your Care Journey
01

Initial Consultation & Assessment

Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or our online form. Our care coordinator conducts a thorough assessment of medical needs, home environment, and patient preferences.

02

Customized Care Plan Creation

Our clinical team collaborates with the patient's physician to develop a personalized care plan that aligns with medical requirements, budget, and scheduling preferences.

03

Caregiver Matching & Introduction

We carefully match the right caregiver based on medical specialization, personality compatibility, and language preference — you meet them before care begins.

04

Ongoing Care & Progress Monitoring

Regular care reviews, health progress reports, and responsive adjustments ensure the care plan evolves with the patient's changing needs and recovery milestones.
