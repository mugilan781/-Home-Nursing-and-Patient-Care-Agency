4. Booking, Modification & Cancellation Policy

4.1 Booking Confirmation

Service bookings are confirmed only upon written confirmation from CareHaven (via email, SMS, or WhatsApp) and receipt of any applicable advance payment. Verbal confirmations do not constitute binding bookings.

4.2 Modification

Booking modifications requested with 24+ hours notice may be accommodated subject to caregiver availability at no additional charge. Modifications with less than 24 hours notice are subject to a ₹200 modification fee.

4.3 Cancellation

  • 48+ hours notice: Full refund of any advance payment

CareHaven reserves the right to cancel bookings with full refund in cases of caregiver illness, safety concerns, force majeure events, or circumstances beyond our control.

CareHaven caregivers are bound by our Code of Professional Conduct, which includes:

  • Maintaining professional boundaries at all times
  • Providing care with dignity, respect, and non-discrimination
  • Adhering to clinical protocols and infection control standards
  • Maintaining strict confidentiality of patient information
  • Reporting incidents, near-misses, and concerns to the CareHaven clinical team immediately
  • Not accepting gifts, loans, or personal favors from clients or families
  • Not engaging in private arrangements or accepting direct employment offers from clients

7. Client Obligations & Safe Environment

Clients are responsible for:
